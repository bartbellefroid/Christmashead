Mutsify 🎅

Upload a photo. Every face — human, cat, dog, cow or horse — gets a Christmas hat. Never the same way twice.

Selected hat

Click Mutsify! again for a new arrangement. Drag a hat to move it, use the mouse wheel to resize (Shift + wheel to tilt), and double-click or double-tap anywhere to add another hat.

Everything happens in your browser — your photo is never uploaded. Face and animal detection by Google MediaPipe; animal heads are estimated from the animal's outline, so drag a hat if it lands on a tail.
